3. **Yell.com**
4. **Thomson Local**
5. **Checkatrade**

Ensure NAP (Name, Address, Phone) is consistent across all citations. Currently the website lists a Turkish phone number (+90 236 214 2590) — ensure the correct UK contact details are used for all UK citations.

## What NOT to Do

- PBNs (Private Blog Networks)
- Automated link building tools
- Mass blog comment links
- Paid links designed purely to pass PageRank
- "Guest posts" on low-quality sites with commercial anchor text
- Link exchanges with unrelated businesses
- Spammy directory submissions
